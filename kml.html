How it works

  • Select two or more KML files from your device.
  • The tool will extract Placemarks, Routes, and Polygons from each file.
  • Each imported file's contents will be placed in its own separate Folder within the merged KML to keep things organized.
  • Click Merge and Download to save the combined file.
